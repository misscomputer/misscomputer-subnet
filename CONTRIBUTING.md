AGPL-3.0-only only. No contributor license agreement, dual-license grant, or
private-reuse grant is requested. Do not submit credentials, private operator
configuration, provider-specific adapters, or private infrastructure code.

By submitting a contribution, you certify that you have the right to provide
it under AGPL-3.0-only and agree that it may be redistributed under those
public terms.
